HOW THE DROP WOULD WORK

1. Pick a template, type the artist and song name, drop in the cut-out photo (with the background remover you already have) and the cover art.
2. Drop in an MP3 / OGG / WAV (the wave dances to the real song) or an MP4 (it plays in the middle frame). Choose the 15, 30 or 60 second part to use.
3. Click RENDER. The video is made right in your browser and saved as an MP4, sized to stay under 25 MB at full HD.
4. Your audio and video files are never uploaded or stored. They only live in the browser while it renders, then they are gone.
